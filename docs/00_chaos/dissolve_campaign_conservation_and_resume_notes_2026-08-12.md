## 1. The Problem We Were Actually Solving

The immediate implementation gap is narrow:

```text
CONNECT and DISSOLVE are registered organs.
CONNECT has executed under opt-in Tree authority.
DISSOLVE has direct local tests and raw/formed mutation paths.
DISSOLVE still lacks a runner-grown rigidity/release life selected by the
qualified body policy without a harness-supplied reason.
```

The deeper problem is larger. The body can create and retain form, but a living
relation machine also needs a lawful way to stop old form from binding the
present forever.

Without that law:

```text
relations accumulate
old commitments keep pressure after their support is gone
encoded form can become permanent merely because it once existed
new insight can be blocked by obsolete structure
```

The desired answer is not unconditional deletion. DISSOLVE must distinguish:

```text
unknown form
temporarily unobserved form
still-supported form
stale or contradicted form
recoverably released form
irreversibly discarded form
```

It must then release only an exact bounded target, preserve provenance, and
account separately for what ceased to constrain the field and what information
was truly lost.

## 5. Why The Measurement Instrument Had To Come First

Before the instrument, a test could appear to prove DISSOLVE while proving only
one of these weaker statements:

```text

```

This project has already encountered the general failure class: code and a
synthetic test can agree with one another while both disagree with a living
run. DISSOLVE is especially vulnerable because a visible released object makes
it tempting to skip the causal history that authorized the release.

The instrument was built to answer a stricter question:

```text

and did that edge physically arrive and execute?
```

Without that answer, changing DISSOLVE from metaphor would be calibration by
story. We could make the organ appear anywhere we wanted and then call its
appearance evidence for the theory that produced the fixture.
