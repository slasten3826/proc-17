## What Was Observed Working

The body has grown complete PLAN lives and narrow create-only BUILD lives. It
has produced real files through the capability-safe hand, independently
verified their bytes and manifested the result. It has also grown a rejected
QA lineage through corpse, carrier, NETWORK rebirth and inherited-form
DISSOLVE without rewriting the ancestor's verdict.

The QA boundary was exercised against replay, stale identity, symlink and root
substitution, malformed trusted results, ambiguous cleanup, receipt/body
splits, provider failures and repeated residue. Harness and infrastructure
faults remain loud instead of being converted into attractive Packet deaths.

substrate adapters. Matched runs showed that proc-17 preserves its authority
boundary while model quality still materially affects semantic output. A

carrier: the raw carrier was correctly rejected, while a deterministic textual
compilation completed one build life. This is experimental evidence, not

## What Proc-17 Is Not

proc-17 is not a finished product or a general coding agent.

Its public build path creates one absent root-level text file. It does not
patch, overwrite, delete, rename, inspect an arbitrary legacy tree, coordinate
multiple files or expose unrestricted shell authority. The QA hand is bounded
and manually entered rather than an automatic product pipeline. Lineage lives
in memory and does not survive a host restart. Full-tree authority remains
opt-in and its promotion corpus is incomplete. General DISSOLVE, recurring
semantic garbage collection, persistent cold-corpus retrieval and
receiver-native cross-model anchors remain unfinished.

The `release/v0.1.0` directory is a narrow release-candidate surface. No Git tag
was created and this conservation record does not promote it into a published
release.

## Why Work Stopped Here

Active development moved to ProcessChainNetwork.

proc-17 remains in this repository as the working experimental machine that we
built, ran, tested and learned from.
